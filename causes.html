THE JOURNEY

How Your Money Moves

From the moment you hit donate, your funds are tracked, allocated, and deployed to create lasting, community-led change. Here is the step-by-step path.

Step 01

Collection & Pooling

Every donation is received and combined with resources globally, deposited in fully transparent, audited bank structures.

Step 02

Community Allocation

Local committees review project needs, submitting vetted requests directly to the board for immediate fund distribution.

Step 03

Local Project Action

Construction supplies are bought from local businesses and communities carry out the physical setups, building self-reliance.

Step 04

Audit & Results

Third-party audits confirm proper deployment, and final impact metrics are shared back to you in quarterly reports.
