ABOUT US

Change moves at the speed of trust.

Stackly Foundation was founded in 2011 by a group of teachers, nurses and neighbors who believed that lasting change is co-authored, not delivered. We fund the people already doing the work.

MISSION

Fund the people closest to the problem.

We move flexible capital, tools and networks to grassroots leaders in 37 countries. We don't parachute in. We stay long enough to become useful, then quiet enough to be forgotten.

Partnership

We don't design programs. We resource the ones already working.

Transparency

Every dollar is trackable. Annual reports are public and plain-language.

Patience

No 90-day pilots. We commit for 5-10 years or we don't commit at all.

OUR TEAM

42 staff. 214 partners.
One shared table.

Our team is spread across nine countries and led locally in every region we work. We are teachers, midwives, engineers, artists, and — most importantly — neighbors.
